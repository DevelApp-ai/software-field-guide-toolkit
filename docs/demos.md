# Demos

CI demonstrates both analysis commands against two public example codebases — [ErpLight](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/ErpLight) (plugin-module ERP) and [RuntimePluggableClassFactory](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/RuntimePluggableClassFactory) (runtime pluggable class factory) — and uploads the generated maps, findings, and ledgers as workflow artifacts.

The tenant-probe demo is in-repo: [`demo/MultitenantApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a small EF Core app with a per-tenant query filter and a fail-closed `SaveChanges` guard, plus two deliberately regressed variants (query filter removed; save guard removed). CI generates the probe suite, runs it green against the good app, then swaps each broken variant in and asserts the suite **fails** — the #58 done-when proof: isolation regressions fail loudly, by construction.

The cockpit demo is also in-repo: [`demo/StranglerApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a small strangler-fig app whose `shipping.quote` route is strangled end to end — promoted at the routing pivot, shadow-diffed green against the retired legacy handler, and retired below the traffic threshold, so the sunset tripwire fires (the #56 done-when: one route strangled with the tripwire armed and firing). CI also runs three deliberately broken variants: a drifted new implementation (the shadow suite fails with `SHADOW DRIFT`), an unwired declared route (`migration/unwired-route` error), and a strangled route whose traffic contradicts the claim (`migration/strangle-unfinished` error).

The events demo is in-repo too: [`demo/EventApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a small eventing app with a compliant `OrderEventsConsumer` (dedup, dead letters, old-schema tolerance) and `Outbox`, two event contracts — `order.placed` v2 with a v1 skew replay, and `invoice.issued` v1 — plus two deliberately regressed variants: a naive consumer (no dedup, crashes on poison, refuses old versions) and a fire-and-forget outbox (publishes vanish, dispatches leave no bookkeeping). CI generates the suite, runs it green against the good app, then swaps each broken variant in and asserts the suite **fails** with `EventConformance.ConsumerProbes` / `EventConformance.OutboxProbes` — the #60 done-when: seeded faults visibly fail a naive consumer and pass a compliant one.

The plan-audit demo is in-repo too: [`demo/PlanAudit`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a small QuoteTree estate with two spec areas (quote, billing) mapped to their code areas and four committed diff pairs: a conformant control, an omission (the billing spec gains "invoices are exportable as PDF" that no generated change implements), an unapproved addition (a surprise fee appears in `InvoiceService`), and a cross-area pair (the audit-trail requirement implemented in a new file outside the map's coverage) that only a labeled semantic match reconciles. CI runs all four — the control is silent, each seeded discrepancy fires with its line-anchored citation, and regeneration is byte-identical — the #64 done-when: the auditor bites on omission and unapproved addition and stays silent on a conformant pair, deterministically.

The eval demo is in-repo too: [`demo/EvalApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a docs-assistant suite with two stages (retrieval scored by `recallAt3`, generation scored by `faithfulness` and `refusalCorrectness`), nine cases — seven human-labeled, one of which the judge disagrees on so the agreement board shows real calibration (6/7 = 0.8571, disagreements named) — a committed baseline, and two deliberately broken recorded runs: a regression (r3 drops out of the top-3, g2's summary drifts — both metrics fall below baseline *and* threshold, `--strict` exits 1 with `eval/baseline-regression` citing the moved cases) and an undeclared case (r99 appears in the run, `eval/unknown-result`). CI runs all three — the control is clean with its calibration visible, the committed baseline is byte-identical to what `--update-baseline` writes, and each seeded failure bites — the #66 done-when: the seeded suite runs in CI, the seeded regression is caught with per-case evidence, judge agreement is reported against the human-labeled sample, and everything reproduces from a clean checkout.

The migration-lint demo is in-repo too: [`demo/MigrationApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a five-migration EF Core estate that walks the expand → backfill → contract sequence by the book — `Orders.ShippingAddress` is replaced by `DeliveryAddress` with a batched, gated, measured window (expanded 2026-03-01, cold 2026-05-25, dropped 2026-06-01, isolated in its own migration) — plus five deliberately broken variants: a naive unbatched backfill (`migration/naive-backfill`), a drop-then-archive that references the dropped column (`migration/destructive-first` + `migration/drop-not-isolated`), an ungated removal of `Orders.LegacyTotal` (`migration/contract-without-gate`), an unmeasured window (no `coldAt` — `migration/removal-without-window`), and a rushed window whose drop runs before the column measured cold (`migration/removal-before-cold`). CI runs the control clean and byte-identical on regeneration, then asserts each variant bites under `--strict` with its cited evidence — the #67 done-when: the control stays silent, each seeded violation is caught by construction, deterministically.

The DORA demo is in-repo too: [`demo/DoraApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a recorded export of a **real** public repository — DevelApp-ai/RuntimePluggableClassFactory's October 2025 release train (v2.0.2 → v2.1.0, 12 releases in 10 days) — with 13 merged changes, 46 CI runs, and the one failure visible in the public history: v2.0.7 broke the GitHub Pages deploy, hotfixed by PR #18 in v2.0.8 (mode `hotfix`, MTTR 2.8 h, evidence verified inside the incident window). v2.0.9 is a direct commit with no PR — an empty `prs` list is legal because the extractor verified it empty, not because it skipped it. Five deliberately broken variants from `demo/broken/` bite under `--strict` via `--events`: a re-run mislabeled as a new deployment (`dora/re-run-mislabeled`, excluded from every count — the #68 done-when), a dangling PR join, an unbuilt release, an unknown restore mode, and unevidenced hotfix evidence. CI runs the control clean with the joins table verified and the committed definitions file byte-identical on regeneration.

The review-calib demo is in-repo too: [`demo/ReviewCalibApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a recorded export of review decisions for a synthetic `example/checkout` repository — 20 reviews across two queues (`checkout`, `infra`) and two roles (`senior`, `junior`), eight seeded defects (all caught in the control), and a recorded judge that disagrees on `rv-005` so the agreement board shows real calibration (0.8 on the judged sample, disagreements named). Three deliberately broken variants from `demo/broken/` bite under `--strict` via `--events`: a seeded defect approved cleanly (`calib/seed-missed` — the #77 done-when, the one miss the instrument exists to catch), a role below the calibration floor (`calib/catch-rate-below` — junior catches 3 of 4, 75.0% against the 80% floor), and a rubber-stamping queue (`calib/rubber-stamp-queue` — checkout approves 12 of 12 at a 0.5 s median, plus `calib/stale-queue` on its unmeasured window). CI runs the control clean with every seed caught and the board byte-identical on regeneration.

The bias-probe demo is in-repo too: [`demo/BiasProbeApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a docs-assistant probe pack on the #66 estate — ten counterfactual pairs (Anna vs Ahmed across tone, hedging, length, and benefitOfTheDoubt), sixteen cases sliced over two region cohorts with a human-labeled sample the judge disagrees on one of (agreement 0.6667, `g3` named), should-succeed and must-refuse cases, retrieval cases with citations matching the declared 0.75/0.25 corpus shares, and a committed baseline. CI runs the control clean (consistency 1.0, byte-identical on regeneration, baseline exactly what `--update-baseline` writes), then asserts five deliberately broken variants from `demo/broken/` bite under `--strict` via `--results`: a marker flip that moves the tone score (`bias/pair-inconsistent` + `bias/consistency-regression` citing `p3` — the #78 done-when, the seeded inconsistency caught with the pair and dimension named), a cohort regression the overall hides (`bias/baseline-regression` + `bias/cohort-baseline-regression` citing `g5` — region-b 0.8167 while the overall 0.8833 sits above the 0.8 threshold), a refusal gap (`bias/refusal-asymmetry` — region-b refuses 2 of 5 against region-a's 0), an answered must-refuse case (`bias/refusal-missed`), and citation skew (advisory: 2 warnings, exit 0 by design).

The drills demo is in-repo too: [`demo/DrillApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a resilience drill estate on the book's own pipeline as the rehearsal target — four scenarios (PDF build restore, kill-switch re-entry, database failover, dependency registry outage), each with declared clocks (alert, RTO, RPO) and expected telemetry signals, and a conformant recorded export (drill `pipeline-drill-2026-10`, asOf 2026-10-15). CI runs the control clean (all clocks met, all signals recorded, every scenario fresh, byte-identical on regeneration), then asserts six deliberately broken variants from `demo/broken/` bite under `--strict` via `--evidence`: a late alert (`drill/late-alert` — d3 alerts 120s over its 60s clock, the #81 done-when), an over-RTO restore (`drill/restore-over-rto` — d1 restores 4200s against a 3600s RTO), a breached RPO (`drill/rpo-breached` — d2 loses 900s against a 600s RPO), a never-drilled scenario (`drill/never-drilled` — d4 vanishes from the export: a paper plan caught), a missing expected signal (`drill/signal-missing`), and a stale scenario (`drill/stale-scenario` — 283 days old, advisory warning, exit 0 by design).

The unit-econ demo is in-repo too: [`demo/UnitEconApp`](https://github.com/DevelApp-ai/software-field-guide-toolkit/blob/main/demo/README.md), a **SEEDED** cloud unit-economics estate — three tenants (acme, globex, initech) on three services (api, render, pipeline) across two months, with each service's allocation shares summing to exactly one and every tenant inside its declared budget (labeled SEEDED in the README and on every board: no real billing history backs these numbers). CI runs the control clean (every join verified, byte-identical on regeneration), then asserts four deliberately broken variants from `demo/broken/` bite under `--strict` via `--billing`/`--allocations`/`--telemetry`: a dangling allocation (an extra rule prices the never-billed `search` — `unitecon/dangling-allocation`, the #83 done-when), an unbilled service (telemetry serves `search` — `unitecon/unbilled-service`), an unpriced tenant (`hooli` serves requests no rule prices — `unitecon/unpriced-tenant`), and a spend spike (api's September line jumps 840 → 1000, pushing only acme over its 650 budget with per-tenant evidence — `unitecon/spend-spike`).
